Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Genial Software Solutions Inc. is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our users' personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you share with us.

Information we collect

We may collect personal information when you visit our website, use our services, or otherwise interact with us — including your name, email address, contact details, and other information relevant to the services we're providing.

How we use it

We use the information we collect to understand your needs and provide a personalized experience: improving our services, responding to inquiries, keeping you updated on relevant news or offers, and customizing our website around your interests.

Information security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and use industry-standard practices to protect it from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Information sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties without your consent. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us deliver our services or run our business — these providers are contractually required to keep your information confidential and use it only for the purposes we specify.

Cookies & tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, analyze traffic, and customize content. You can accept or decline cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect some site functionality.

External links

Our site may link to external websites. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites, and we'd encourage you to review their policies before sharing any personal information with them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; changes will be reflected on this page, and we'd encourage checking back periodically.
